Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11/3/2026. This Privacy Policy explains what MoCo Sign up (mocosignup.com), operated by Everett Clark's SaaS, collects, why, and what choices you have. Questions: help@mocosignup.com.

What we collect

  • Name and email address — when you sign up for an event, so the organizer can run the event and contact attendees about it.
  • Profile information you choose to add — when you create or edit a profile, to pre-fill your sign-ups and personalize the service.
  • Event content (title, place, description) — when you post an event, to display the event and to run the checks in the next section.
  • Account information — when an organization creates administrator accounts, to provide and bill for administrator features.
  • Basic technical information — automatically, when you use the site, to operate, secure, and improve the site.

Posting needs no account

Browsing and signing up need no account. Posting an event needs no account. If you do create a profile, your profile and sign-ups are stored securely and privately on the server — not only on your device — so the service can show real sign-up counts and let organizers manage their events.

Automated checks on event text

Event text you submit is checked by our built-in checker and by an automated moderation service provided by OpenAI. Event text is sent to that service for the moderation check. We do not use attendee names or email addresses to train artificial intelligence models, and we do not sell personal information to anyone.

Who sees what

  • Other visitors see event listings and the number of people going. They do not see sign-up names or email addresses.
  • The organizer of an event can see the names and email addresses of people who signed up for that organizer’s event, so the organizer can run it.
  • Administrator account holders for an organization can export that organization’s events and attendee information.
  • Service providers that host the site and its database, provide email, and provide moderation process information only as needed to run the service.
  • We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of a person or the service.

We never sell personal information

MoCo Sign up never sells personal information — not attendee names or email addresses, not profile information, and not any other personal information collected through the service — to anyone, for money or for any other benefit. We also do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. Any statement in an older version of this policy, a dialog on the site, or another document that conflicts with this section is replaced by this section.

Institutions own their attendee data — data sovereignty

When a library branch, recreation center, or other organization (an “Institution”) uses MoCo Sign up for its events, the attendee and user information collected through that Institution’s events — including sign-up names, email addresses, and sign-up records for those events — belongs to the Institution. The Institution has full data sovereignty over that information: it owns the data, and MoCo Sign up does not own it.

  • MoCo Sign up acts only as a processor (service provider) on the Institution’s behalf for Institution attendee data. We process it only to provide the service and on the Institution’s instructions, as set out in the Data Processing Agreement.
  • We will not use Institution attendee data for our own marketing, advertising, product training, or any purpose unrelated to providing the service to that Institution, and we will never sell it.
  • On the Institution’s request, and in any event at the end of a pilot or paid plan, we will return that Institution’s attendee data to it (by spreadsheet/CSV export) and then delete it from our systems, except where retention is required by law.
  • An Institution’s data sovereignty does not change an individual attendee’s own rights in their personal information, including the right to ask for deletion as described below.

What we do not do

  • We never sell your information.
  • We do not use attendee information for advertising.
  • We do not require an email verification step for a basic event sign-up beyond what the sign-up form itself asks for.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep sign-up information for as long as needed to run the relevant event and the organizer’s records, and then delete or de-identify it. Cancelling a sign-up deletes that sign-up record. You may ask us to delete your profile or personal information by emailing help@mocosignup.com; we will respond within a reasonable time and delete it except where we must keep it by law. Organizations on pilot or paid plans may request an export of their events and attendee information as described in their agreement.

Security

Profiles, events, and sign-ups are stored on a hosted server database with access controls, and administrator secrets and moderation keys are kept server-side, not in public website code. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit access to personal information to what the service needs.

Children

MoCo Sign up is a community events service for the general public and is not directed to children under 13. Do not submit a child’s personal information through the service except as needed by a parent or guardian to register that child for an event, where the organizer permits it.

Your choices and changes to this policy

You may browse without an account, decline to add optional profile information, and cancel a sign-up at any time. We may update this policy; the version published on the site with its effective date controls, and material changes will be announced on the site.
