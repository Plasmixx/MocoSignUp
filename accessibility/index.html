Accessibility

MoCo Sign up is built to be easy to use for everyone, including older residents. You do not need an account to browse events or to sign up for one.

How the site helps

  • Large, readable text — body text is large by default, with clear headings and high-contrast light and dark themes you can switch in Settings.
  • Keyboard and screen reader support — buttons and forms are labelled, and the site can be used without a mouse.
  • Reduced motion — if your device is set to reduce motion, animations are turned off.
  • Accessible events filter — use the Accessible filter to show only wheelchair-accessible events, and event pages list accessibility features such as seating, free parking, public transit, and hearing loops where the organizer provides them.
  • No extra logins — signing up does not require creating an account or remembering another password.
